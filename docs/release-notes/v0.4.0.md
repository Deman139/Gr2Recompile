## 🎚️ FSR / DLSS quality modes (new in the launcher)

*Launcher → Graphics → **FSR / DLSS quality***. The 3D scene is drawn **smaller** and FSR 3.1 or DLSS
rebuild it up to your output resolution. **HUD, menus and videos stay sharp at full size.**

| Mode | Scene resolution at **1080p** | at **1440p** | at **1800p** | at **4K** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Native** (best quality) | 1080p | 1080p | 1080p | 1080p |
| **Quality** | 720p | 960p | 1080p | 1080p |
| **Balanced** | 630p | 836p | 1044p | 1080p |
| **Performance** | 540p | 720p | 900p | 1080p |
| **Ultra performance** | 360p | 480p | 600p | 720p |

| ✅ Use it when | ❌ Don't bother when |
|---|---|
| Your **graphics card is the limit**: modest GPU, or playing at 1440p / 1800p / 4K and the GPU sits at ~100% | Your **processor is the limit** (low FPS while the GPU is not at 100%): drawing fewer pixels doesn't help the CPU |
| You want more FPS and accept a slightly softer image | Your GPU already holds your target FPS: **Native** looks best |

- It needs **FSR 3.1 or DLSS** as the resolution method (*Advanced → Resolution method*) or **frame
  generation** turned on. With the classic resolution patch it doesn't apply.
- It's read when the game starts: change it in the launcher before pressing PLAY.
- The in-game menu (**Insert → Image**) shows the real sizes in use: *"WxH scene, WxH output"*.
- ℹ️ In our own tests on a CPU-limited PC the FPS gain was small, as expected. This is a feature
  for GPU-limited PCs.

## 🧩 Shader cache and precompiling (launcher + in-game)

Killzone's shaders are **compiled by your PC the first time each area needs them**. That's why a
new zone can stutter, or an effect can appear a moment late. Each compiled shader is saved in the
**cache**, so **once you've played an area, it's prepared**: the next time it loads fast.

| Where | Option | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🧰 Launcher → Advanced | **Precompile shaders at startup** *(on by default)* | Before the game opens, it prepares **every shader already in your cache**, using all your cores. The start takes longer (the first time with a big cache, more) and uses more memory while it works. |
| 🎮 In game → Performance | **Compile new shaders in the background** | Live, no restart. Fewer stutters in **new** areas; an object or effect may show up a moment later. |

- 💡 **Who benefits most:** PCs with a **not-so-fast CPU**. The work is done once, at startup,
  instead of while you play, so there are far fewer hitches in-game.
- ⏳ **The first time can take a while**, and areas you **never played** still compile as you reach
  them. After that, starts are quick. The more you play, the better it gets.
- 🛠️ Fixed: some shaders failed to be saved ("code is too big") and were rebuilt again and again;
  damaged cache entries are now cleaned automatically.
- 🗑️ The cache can take several GB: **Clear cache** is in the launcher.

## 🎬 Screen effects: turn them off (new)

*Launcher → Graphics → **Effects** · in game: Insert → Image → **Effects***

| Effect | Applies |
|---|---|
| Motion blur · Depth of field · Bloom · Film grain | 🔄 **On game start.** Change them in the launcher before PLAY, or in the in-game menu and press **Apply and restart** |

## ⌨️ Keyboard and mouse button icons in the game (new)

The game's prompts ("Press ✕ to open…") now show **your keys** instead of PS4 buttons, in menus, pause
tabs, the OWL, melee and the touchpad.

| Option | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Last device used** *(default)* | Keys while you use mouse and keyboard, PS4 buttons if you grab a controller |
| **Keyboard and mouse** | Always your keys (or the mouse button) |
| **PS4** | Always the original icons |

- The icons follow **your key bindings** (launcher → *Controls*). Sticks and the touchpad keep their
  PS4 icon.
- 🔄 **Restart to change it:** the game uses the option it was **opened with**. If you change it
  in the in-game menu (**Insert → Controls → Button prompts**), use **Apply and restart**.

## 🖼️ Other improvements

- **Texture sharpness (LOD bias)** now goes from **-2 to +2** and only affects the world's
  materials, no longer bloom, depth of field or particles. **Anisotropic filtering** no longer
  touches buffers that must stay unfiltered.
- The launcher tabs **scroll** (bar and mouse wheel) when the window is small.
- The in-game overlay (Insert) shows the scene and output resolutions.
- New diagnostics and several internal fixes.

## 🧩 Caché de shaders y precompilado (launcher + dentro del juego)

Los shaders de Killzone **los compila tu PC la primera vez que cada zona los necesita**. Por eso una
zona nueva puede dar tirones, o un efecto puede aparecer un momento tarde. Cada shader compilado se
guarda en la **caché**, así que **una vez jugada una zona, queda preparada**: la próxima vez carga rápido.

| Dónde | Opción | Qué hace |
|---|---|---|
| 🧰 Launcher → Avanzado | **Precompilar shaders al iniciar** *(activada por defecto)* | Antes de abrir el juego prepara **todos los shaders que ya están en tu caché**, usando todos tus núcleos. El inicio tarda más (la primera vez con una caché grande, más aún) y usa más memoria mientras trabaja. |
| 🎮 En el juego → Performance | **Compile new shaders in the background** | En vivo, sin reiniciar. Menos tirones en zonas **nuevas**; un objeto o efecto puede aparecer un momento después. |

- 💡 **Quién más lo aprovecha:** las PCs con un **procesador no muy rápido**. El trabajo se hace una
  sola vez, al iniciar, en lugar de mientras jugás, y hay muchos menos tirones dentro del juego.
- ⏳ **La primera vez puede tardar**, y las zonas que **nunca jugaste** se siguen compilando al llegar.
  Después los inicios son rápidos. Cuanto más jugás, mejor queda.
- 🛠️ Corregido: algunos shaders no se guardaban ("code is too big") y se reconstruían una y otra vez;
  las entradas dañadas de la caché ahora se limpian solas.
- 🗑️ La caché puede ocupar varios GB: **Borrar caché** está en el launcher.
